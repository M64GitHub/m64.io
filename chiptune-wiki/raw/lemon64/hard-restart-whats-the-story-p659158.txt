--- encore — 2015-02-02T07:48:11+00:00
flavioweb: With 'reset' (I don't think it's called 'hard reset' though?) you're manipulating the test bit. 'Hard restart' is a technique to avoid that the envelope bug occurs. So yes, different subjects.

commodorejohn: As far as I know it's completely deterministic from an emulators point of view and it's quite well explained in envelope.h, around line 118. It's another thing to be deterministic about it from a 6502-programmers standpoint though. The routes I use are either

1. use 'hard restart'

2. finding safe ADSR-values in a song where the bug never triggers (and this depends entirely on the situation/arrangment/instrument/bpm)

3. keep a voice continously in it's sustain mode and simulating new notes with changes in pwm or filter instead.
