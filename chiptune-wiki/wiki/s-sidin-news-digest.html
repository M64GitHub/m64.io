The News section of every sidin issue reported the releases of the SID scene "from an issue to another": editors, players, emulation libraries, hardware, competitions and collections. This page condenses all fifteen sections into a dated timeline by topic — one line per item, giving the date the news gives (or the issue's date, marked #n, when it gives none) — and collects what the editorials say about the magazine itself. Names and version numbers are as printed; nothing is added from outside the issues.

The magazine itself (from the editorials)#

  • #1 (2002-09): "This is the first issue of a Sid related magazine", born "to give some feedback about sid activity to other sid people"; the news items are "to be considerate evergreen"; written in OpenOffice for "an open archive format … available on most of the platforms" that allows images, distributed as .sxw and PDF; no schedule — "I cannot say when the next issue will be out".
  • Rights: #2 "the right of the presented code remain to the two authors" (David Whittaker and Matt Gray — "Matt! Where are you?"); #3 "Andrew Colin and Commodore"; #4 "the copyright of the presented codes remain to the original authors: contact them for a business use of it".
  • Hosting: Tognon's own site digilander.iol.it/ice00/tsid/ (later digilander.libero.it) hosted the issues, a revision.txt of fixed errata (#4: "I have already made the version 1.1 of each issue with some corrections"), the SidWine, Tiny Sid and Big Sid compos and HVMEC; from #15 the new page is iceteam.altervista.org/sidin.php (the Ice Team site announced in #14).
  • Format changes: #9 switched from .sxw to .odt (OpenOffice 1.5/2.0) and started colouring the 6502 listings through KWrite's syntax highlighting.
  • Why the gaps: #3 "the holiday, but the most reason is the too much activity"; #5 a month late — no Christmas holiday, a Linux distribution rebuild, "lot of activity in the engines"; #6 released early because of a minigame competition "until September"; #8 "a lot in delay as I want to release it in May"; #11 fifteen days without Internet in December 2006 and a modem that needed "15 minutes of trying only for sending or receiving a mail"; #12 "a very delayed issue and a very soft one" (XSidplay2, HVMEC and a Java tracker in progress); #13 "the previous number was released in middle 2008" — the time went into JITT64; #14 "the last issue of SIDin was released more than 4 years ago" (JITT64, XSidPlay2's Qt4 port, HVMEC's move to hvmec.altervista.org at "73% of completion"); #15 planned for the end of May, released at Halloween.
  • Editorial notes worth keeping: #3 offers to reverse-engineer readers' favourite engines on request; #7 floats the idea of a tiny-SID competition ("why not making a competition about writing very smal sid? A mix about programming and sid composition"); #8 "Martin Galway had read the article about Arkanoid in SidIn #4 and found it interesting"; #10 prints Frantic's correction of #9's analysis of his 256-byte player; #11 the SID Factory and NinjaTracker 2 comparison exists because both "were released in the same week"; #12 announces the Java tracker with instrument definitions of "up to 2KB of data" (jitt64); #13 Tognon found Wally Beben on Facebook; #14 "Matt Gray is again in the scene!!"; #15 PSID files "were extended to allow 3 SID chips", and the Little Sara Sister game is moving from the C64 to Java.

Key claims — the timeline by topic#

GoatTracker (goattracker, lasse-oorni)#

  • 1.33 (#1, 2002-09): "the music editor by Lasse Öörni for cross develop"; "from version 1.3 the 12 raster time lines is not be granted anymore", multispeed since 1.24, Linux through SDL (screenshot title "GOATTRACKER 01.33 (reSID + SDL)"); covertbitops.cjb.net/tools/goattrk.zip.
  • 1.4b Final and a stereo version (gstereo.zip), 2003-01-09 (#3): "a programmable (1 or 2 frame) hard restart and better steps programmable filter", a tweak utility and a GoatTracker→NinjaTracker converter.
  • 1.4 for Mac OS X, 2003-04-13 (#4): reSID or Catweasel output.
  • 1.4c 2003-09-20 and 1.4d a month later (#5): mono and stereo builds "updated for better hardrestart handling (wavetable also executed on hardrestart frame)" and manual fade in/out; 1.4d fixes $D418 and arpeggio execution; 1.4e (December 2003) reSID 0.15; 1.4f (January 2004) Funktempo.
  • 1.5, 2004-01-25 (#6): "Playroutine rewritten. Uses testbit hard restart from now on for much sharper sounds"; hard-restart ADSR configurable ("previously only AD"); orderlist cut/copy/paste; delayed wavetable execution; proper gate-off; master fader command 7F0–7FF, timing mark now 7EF; the standard playroutine saves its zero-page locations. 1.51 2004-02-16 (funktempo hack changed), 1.52 April 2004 (better-tuned frequency table; relocation address irrelevant for gamemusic tunes).
  • 1.53, May 2005 (#8): packer fix for instruments without hard restart/pulse init.
  • 2.0, 2005-06-23 (#8): "extends the version 1.xx and break the compatibility with file format" — "more commands and uniform step programming tables for waveform/arpeggio, pulse effects, and filter effects"; 63 instruments; reSID interpolation /I, hex row display /D, BETACONV; "Pulse modulation speeds are doubled" (RC1). 2.01 under-I/O gamemusic routine; 2.03 wavetable right-side $80 keeps the frequency, relocator strips unused data; 2.04 a "questionable 25Hz mode (/S0)"; 2.05 instrument legato (Hardres/1stwave 00), ADSR writes moved away from wave writes; 2.07 PAL/NTSC and 6581/8580 flags per PSID v2NG; 2.08 finevibrato /V; 2.09 Minimal playroutine; 2.1, 2005-07-30: SHIFT+N negates speeds, no-funktempo/no-octave-0 optimisations ("8 sub-types for each playroutine"), maximum speed 16×.
  • 2.12–2.18, August–November 2005 (#9): buffered SID writes (2.12/2.13); 2.18 "Wavetable left side values changed. Delay is now $01-$0F and inaudible waveforms (register values $00-$0F) have been mapped to table values $E0-$EF."
  • 2.2, 2005-12-11 (#9): the speedtable "for more precise control of vibrato, portamento and funktempo", SHIFT+O/SHIFT+R/SHIFT+RETURN converters, "Song and instrument format modified for 4 tables"; 2.21 2005-12-21 (NTSC CIA timer fix), 2.22 2006-01-09.
  • 2.23–2.35 (#10): SHIFT+F5/F6 multiplier, SHIFT+F7 HR ADSR, SHIFT+F8 SID model (2.23–2.24); relocator error messages, a Mac OS X build (2.25); "optimize everything" on SHIFT+ESC (2.26); mouse control (2.28); 2.29beta "new assembler-based relocator that leaves out unused portions of player code" — "one can choose combinations of player features" instead of a player version, SHIFT+H "hifi" speedtable values (2.29); /O disables pulse skipping and tempo 2 is allowed (2.31); pattern length 128 (2.32); instrument 63's attack/decay sets the default tempo (2.33); 1–2-channel relocator optimisation (2.34).
  • 2.4–2.5 (#10): "one bit of accuracy to pulse modulation speed" with a new song format GTS4, pattern commands executable from the wavetable (2.4); shrink/expand and join/split (2.43); /Rxx (2.45); "experimental random delay to write of reSID register, in order to make potential ADSR-bugging on C64 audible" (/Z, 2.47); /G guard (2.49).
  • 2.51–2.59 (#11): gatetimer high bit to start the wavetable on the init frame (2.51, removed again in 2.58); SHIFT+SPACE (2.52); /F custom SID clock (2.56); 2.57 "alternative hardrestart method & playroutine that is used when HR attack parameter is at maximum (FF00 or F800 for example) - this can in theory give better reliability"; 2.59 format GTS5/GTI5, gate-off timer bits $80 (HR disable) and $40 (gate-off disable), 1stFrame wave $01–$8F, $00, $FE/$FF; cadaver.homeftp.net/tools/goattrk2.zip.
  • Stereo and HardSID builds: Raf's GT 1.53 stereo for HardSID, November 2006 (#11); "stereo experimental version of Goattracker 2.59", 2007-04-12 (#12); 2.66beta "Initial cycle-exact HardSID support (Win32 only)" and 2.67 configurable HardSID buffer (/T, /U) (#12); Goat Tracker 2SID Mono, "a simple hack of Goatracker 2.70 made by Raf that output mono sound of the 2 SIDs" (#14, CSDb 94423).
  • 2.6–2.65, April 2007 – May 2008 (#12): 800×600, BME library dropped (2.6); backquote channel/table select, SHIFT+number mutes (2.61); 2.62 "realtime calculated note independent (hifi) portamento & vibrato. Warning: has potential for huge rastertime increase".
  • At SourceForge (#13): sourceforge.net/projects/goattracker2 with svn; 2.68: "SID register write order tweaked to resemble JCH NewPlayer 21", unbuffered routine timed like the buffered one, "New reSID-fp engine (with distortion & nonlinearity) from Antti Lankila integrated" (/I 2 and 3), Simon Bennett's command quick reference; 2.69: reSID click fix, newest reSID-fp, filter parameters in the configuration file.

NinjaTracker (ninjatracker)#

  • 1.0, 2002-11-01 (#2): "a new tracker by Lasse Öörni (Cadaver)" using "only 10 rasterline" and very little memory, "for demo programs where memory and rasterlines are limited"; 1.01 two weeks later, 1.02 some days after — "only 8 rasterlines even during badlines".
  • 1.04, 2003-03-05 (#4): the filter command is back; a gamemusic version "that doesn't save playroutine with music (playroutine separately as source code, can play music from any address) and doesn't lie about the rastertime anymore"; GoatTracker→NinjaTracker converter 1.1.
  • 1.05, 2004-01-06 (#5): a slide duration calculator. 1.1, 2004-01-25 (#6): "Hardrestart is more solid (set both AD,SR to $00)" in both standard and gamemusic versions; INS in the pulse and filter tables inserts a 00,00 row (the wavetable inserts 90,00); pointers adjusted on INS/DEL; faster pattern movement.
  • 2.0–2.03, August–September 2006 (#11): 2.0 "commands (also used as instruments), 2-column tables and a slide function that stops at target pitch", saving either executable music data or gamemusic data without the player; 2.01 editor and sound-effect playback improvements; 2.02 two-frame hard restart and zero-page use cut to 2 bytes ("slightly slower and bigger"); 2.03 "hifi" style hard restart, a more optimised routine, note duration range 3–65 with no limit on a pattern's last note. Compared with SID Factory in the same issue's article.

JCH NewPlayer, SID Factory, SIDId (laxity, chordian, sid-factory-ii, sidid)#

  • JCH New Player 21.g4 Final, January 2006 (#10): "the player coded by Laxity to use with JCH" — fixed "a bug in the vibrato feel, that caused an overflow and made the note go out of tune" and "the synchronization (initialization) bug" (CSDb 14838).
  • 21.g5 Final, May 2006 (#10): fixed "the periodical retrig bug, in liveplay mode"; "Implemented stop ($fe in pattern list). Forgot that completely in pre .g5. DOH!" (CSDb 22927).
  • SID Factory 0.05 (alpha 1) (#11, 2007-02-14): "a new editor system, developed by Laxity of Vibrants/Maniacs of Noise. The editor has inherited a lot of features and look from the JCH editor. It's been programmed from scratch and is very different from the JCH editor behind the surface"; loadable players v5 (multispeed) and v6 (low rasterline); m-studios.dk.
  • SIDId 1.0x, September 2006 (#11): "a small DOS utility letting you know which playroutine is used in a SID file. You can add playroutine signatures manually to sidid.cfg"; 1.01 AND function, 1.02 multiscan, 1.03 listing of unidentified files and scanning of all files, 1.04 search for one specific player, 1.05 no-recurse option, 1.06 directory argument, -c config file, -?, SIDIDCFG environment variable; cadaver.homeftp.net/tools/sidid.zip. Adopted by XSIDPLAY2 2.0.0 ("SidId v1.7 support", #12), ACID 64 Player Pro 3.0.0 (May 2009, "SidID search in properties menu", #13) and Sidplay/w 2.6 (2011, "SidId support (latest sidid.cfg included …), player routine detected is displayed in info page / Ctrl-P", #14).

Practical takeaways#

  • The news dates the hard-restart lineage of the Covert BitOps editors: programmable 1–2-frame HR (GT 1.4b, 2003-01), test-bit HR "for much sharper sounds" (GT 1.5, 2004-01), AD and SR both $00 (NinjaTracker 1.1, 2004-01), the alternative routine when the HR attack is at maximum (GT 2.57, 2006), timer bits $80/$40 (GT 2.59), "hifi" HR (NinjaTracker 2.03, 2006) — the background to hard-restart.
  • The wavetable value scheme on goattracker's page ($01–$0F delays, $E0–$EF inaudible waveforms, $80 keep frequency, speedtable) dates from 2.03–2.18 (2005) and 2.2 (2005-12-11); "one bit of accuracy to pulse modulation speed" arrived with 2.4 (2006) and the note-independent speeds with 2.62 (2007–08).
  • Emulation caveats with dates: the ADSR-delay bug in reSID was only fully fixed in 0.15/0.16 (2003–04); the 6581's filter distortion and cutoff curve were first modelled in 2007 (Lankila) and reached GoatTracker in 2.68 as reSID-fp; a PAL frame is $4CC7 cycles (50.125 Hz), which HVSC used in 2004 to re-time ~500 multispeed tunes.
  • The format milestones in one line: PSIDv2NG (HVSC 5.0, 2002-08-17) → RSID (update 33, 2002-10-26) → the C64 BASIC flag (2004) → songlength database inside HVSC (42, 2005-06) → PSID v3 / 2SID and 32-character fields (55, 2011-06-25) → 3SID (2015) — see sid-format.
  • Tiny players are a discipline of their own: 256- and 512-byte SIDs from the two Tiny Sid compos are analysed in issues 8–12 (tiny-sid-compo).
