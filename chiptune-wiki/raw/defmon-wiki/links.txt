===== Info on the SID chip =====

The sid chip is the sound chip of the Commodore 64. It exists in two main revisions, called 6581 and 8580, and there are some sub-revisions as well. 

  * [[http://codebase64.org/doku.php?id=base:sid_programming|Info on SID programming for coders]] - on Codebase64
