SID-Wizard — the WTF licence, quoted from the source package
============================================================

The modules under songs/swm/ are the example tunes shipped with
SID-Wizard 1.97 (resources/examples/).  SID-Wizard is by Hermit
(Mihaly Horvath).  Its README.txt ends:


    2026 Hermit (Mihaly Horvath) with WTF licence
    (Do what the frick you want with this code, but mentioning me if
    you use it would still be nice.)


So: mentioned, gladly.  Hermit wrote SID-Wizard, and DUET's SID-Wizard
side — the .swm/.sws/.swt/.swq loader, the player, the export — is a
port of his 6502 driver, researched from those sources.  Several of the
example tunes are his own; the others carry their composer's name in
the module's own AUTHOR field, and that name is what the gallery shows.
