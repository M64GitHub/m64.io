TITLE: The Kinda Sorta Supersaw-lite; or, a Beefy Saw Bass.
URL: https://roboctopus-8bit.tumblr.com/post/61515601352
DATE: Tue, 17 Sep 2013 14:49:00

The standard saw waveform that the LSDJ WAV synth uses is pretty good sounding. I usually tweak the shape by hand to get a sharper sound and have been happy with it, but I wanted to try and create a supersaw sound in LSDJ.

The idea behind the original supersaw was to make one oscillator sound like 7 (!) detuned saw waves.  It might theoretically be possible to do this in LSDJ by hand drawing slightly out-of-sync waveforms and using frame switching in the WAV channel, but the WAV channel synth is a bit noisy when switching frames, so it doesn&rsquo;t sound very clean. I&rsquo;m going to keep experimenting with that. 

Nevertheless, I did come up with a hand-drawn waveform based on the shape of the supersaw waveform, and it makes a great bass patch with good treble definition and a nice low end that will make the ladies froth and the guys tremble. Or something like that.

As a side note to this, if you hear a cool waveform you want to replicate in LSDJ, an easy way to achieve it is to do a google image search (I searched &ldquo;supersaw waveform&rdquo; haha) and just try to approximate the shape in the WAV screen. You may come up with some interesting results.
