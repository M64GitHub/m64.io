Scarzix just put online the first part of his video tutorial serie for CheeseCutter SID music editor (Windows/Linux).
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gjlIN6ehqqI
CheeseCutter is a great editor on par with Goat Tracker and Sid Duzz It. It's a project of Abaddon, who is also a badass chipmusician, with whom I've co-operated numerous times.
Grab the editor here: http://theyamo.kapsi.fi/ccutter/
There's also a Facebook group for CheeseCutter if you need help with the editor or just wanna share your tunes, etc
https://www.facebook.com/groups/529257460474557/
(Also check out the tunes Scarzix has been making with CheeseCutter: https://soundcloud.com/scarzix)
