 member of Jade and Night55.  Rage burst into the scene when he earned
 2nd place in the Assembly '95 Multichannel music competition.  He has
 consistantly released quality songs, so check out some of his works.

 Rage's new tune, "Superpop", in the Night 55 Musicdisk,  Grandeur.  One 
 final note:>,  Rage talks of his bud Elwood in the interview and how he 
 hasnt released anything on internet yet.  Apparently, Rage brought up 
 several Elwood tunes to the internet, so pick them up on ftp.cdrom.com.

--[2. Faces in the Crowd]-------------------------------------------[Zinc]--
 
    "It's howdy-dooty time!"  Well not really. Actually, it's Faces In
 The Crowd Time.  I conducted an interview with fisherman, who I hadn't
 heard of before.  Hopefully it's easy to read. I think I need to format
 these better. But without further ado...
 
 
 <fisherman> hi.
 
 <zinc> How are you today?
 
 <fisherman> Hello zinc.. Howz things?
 
 <zinc> Fine :)
 
 <fisherman> I am fine... just got back from buying some cds.
 
 <zinc> okay cool, well lets get on with this interview. First of all,
        where are you from?
 
 <fisherman> I am an Australian, but I have lived in Thailand for the
             past 4 years of my life :)
 
 <zinc> Wow, do they have a Thai tracking scene?
 
 <fisherman> The Thai tracking scene consists of me and another aussie
             tracker called Cand-ice (of Moonblade).
 <fisherman> That is all.
 <fisherman> It really sucks, because there is nobody to really talk to
             about tracking..
 
 <zinc> So then, what is your name, age, and all that?
 
 <fisherman> My real name is Matt Overington. I have survived life on
             this planet for 18 years.. I am going to go back to
             Australia for university in a couple of weeks :). That'll
             give me more time to track..
 <fisherman> I need more time to track. That is the worst thing about
             school here (I get up at 5:30am, and get home at 4:00pm).
             I am dead tired the whole time.
 
 <zinc> What is your tracking history?  How did you get started, and
        what groups have you been in/are in?
 
 <fisherman> Well. I started about 3 years ago.. I got my first internet
             account, and decided to get some music for my new
             soundblaster (!). I had a friend in australia that was a
             tracker on the Amiga (he has since retired).
 <fisherman> I got a copy of modedit (didn't EVERYONE start on modedit).
             After that I switched to Scream Tracker 3, and now I use
             Impulse tracker.
 <fisherman> I really started because my life was pretty boring, and I
             needed a way to vent my frustrations about moving to Bangkok.
 <fisherman> I am in a group called MindTap, run by Subliminal.. I only
             joined them a couple of weeks back, so I have not had any
             major releases yet.
 
 <zinc> There's one thing about the scene, its accessible from almost
        anywhere (even Thailand!), and stays the same.
 
 <fisherman> Yep. That is the best thing about the scene. All you
             really need is a computer and a net account (with access
             to irc :)
 
 <zinc> What is that group, MindTap, all about?  (here's yourc hance to
        advertise it!)
 
 <fisherman> Ok, then!!! Here goes a shameless plug about my new group!
 <fisherman> Sub had been advertising about his group (AMP) in his
             modules that are sitting on ftp.cdrom.com.
 <fisherman> I downloaded these files, and decided that since I currently
             wasn't really in a group seriously, I might as well apply to
             join.
 <fisherman> After I sent some mail to him, we decided to change the name
             to MindTap because nobody was interested in AMP (it was
             really cheesy anyhow - ATOMIC MUSIC PRODUCTIONS).
 <fisherman> =)
 
 <zinc>  hehe
 <zinc> Does the group focus on any particular goal or style?
 
 <fisherman> We are setting up for some music disks, and Sub is going
             to compete in a music compo.
 <fisherman> I am really into guitar/piano/orchestral toons. I think that
             I share this taste with Subliminal (although he has released
             some dance tracks under AMP).
 <fisherman> I think that MindTap will develop with time.. It is too soon
             to pick a "definite style" for us yet..
 <fisherman> Give us some time, and then make up your own minds :)
 
 <zinc> What do you think about super-groups like Kosmic or the late
        Epinicion that have a lot of members?
 
 <fisherman> I have almost completed my debut tune for MindTap.. It is
             guitarry and mellow.. I quite like the way it is fitting
             together. . It'll be on ftp.cdrom.com soonish..
 <fisherman> Well. in response to your question about the MegaGroups.. 
 <fisherman> I think that there are a lot of super talented trackers out
             there.. Basehead and Maelcum immediately come to mind..
             They are famous :)
 <fisherman> I think that Kosmic and Epinicion have/had a lot of respect
             from all the trackers in the scene.
 <fisherman> The problem with a young group like MindTap is trying to get
             famous(ish). We will keep putting out the tunes and hope
             that someone listens and likes them..
 <fisherman> Kosmic and Epi were and are great for the scene.. I think
             that they deserve every tracker's admiration.
 <fisherman> You never know.. Perhaps mindtap will be BIG one day, too =)
 
 <zinc> Do you think the scene is getting _too_ big?
 
 <fisherman> The scene is expanding rapidly, and eventually it may get
             too big to really deal with properly..
 <fisherman> #trax still has a "bar-like" atmosphere, and it is really
             friendly.. Since #trax is a fair representation of the
             scene, I think that it is good at the moment. It may
             develop to be unbelievably huge, but it is ok at the moment.
 
 <zinc> Do you think there's a way to deal with the problem of the scene
        getting too big?  Not to mention the number of releases from
        people who really shouldn't be releasing!
 
 <fisherman> That is a problem - untalented trackers pumping out the
             tunes... That has got to stop.
 <fisherman> I don't know. I think that it is really hard to put a limit
             on the scene.. Who is going to oversee everything and say
             "YOU can't write music anymore". That is censorship (and
             something that has been deemed evil by the masses :).
 <fisherman> I think that the skilled musicians will prevail. The
             unskilled ones will either improve or cease to track..
             It will sort itself out, I think.
 
 <zinc> As for the untalented trackers, see my article "The Blacklist"
        in TW 58  :)
 <zinc> Anyways, where would you like the scene to go the next few
        years?  Is there anything you would like to see change?
 
 <fisherman> In the next few years the speeds of access to the net will
             improve. This will allow bigger modules to be composed. I
             think that it will get out of hand eventually, and a
             standard will eventually be reached..
 
 <zinc> Well, the GUS PnP has a hardware limit of 8megs!
 
 <fisherman> I think that the scene will get much bigger, but it will
             not be too bad for another couple of years..
 
 <fisherman> You've got to love the guys at Gravis :) They know what
             they are doing. =)
 
 <zinc> Well, their WHQ is very close to where I live!
 
 <fisherman> I assume that you have one, then?
 
 <zinc> just an ACE.. but anyways, on with the interview.  I'm going to

        answer them as you see fit.

 <fisherman> Go for it :)

 <fisherman> Fast Tracker would be a Watermellon because they are hard

 <fisherman> ScreamTracker would have to be an apple - Kind of boring,

 <fisherman> Well. If I HAVE to keep it clean :)
